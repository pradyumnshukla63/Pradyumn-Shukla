# DHURANDHAR: THE COLD ARCHIVE
### A Cinematic Espionage Scrollytelling Experience
**Directed by Aditya Dhar | Starring Ranveer Singh, Akshaye Khanna, Sanjay Dutt, R. Madhavan, Arjun Rampal**

---

## 📽️ Project Overview
**"Dhurandhar: The Cold Archive"** is an interactive, high-stakes scrollytelling web experience that plunges users into the shadowy world of South Asian geopolitical espionage. Based on the major Hindi-language spy action-thriller duology directed by **Aditya Dhar** (*Uri: The Surgical Strike*), the experience tracks India's deepest undercover intelligence operation in the ganglands of Lyari, Karachi.

---

## 🧭 Narrative Scrollytelling Journey (The Chapters)

1. **Prologue / The Catalyst (1999–2001)**
   - *Dec 1999:* The IC-814 Kandahar hijacking and the scars of forced concession.
   - *Dec 2001:* The attack on the Indian Parliament and the shift in national security doctrine.
   - *The Directive:* Intelligence Bureau Director Ajay Sanyal (R. Madhavan) authorizes **Project Dhurandhar**—infiltrating the adversary's nest from within.

2. **Chapter II: The Erasure (The Metamorphosis)**
   - Captain Jaskirat Singh Rangi (Ranveer Singh) has his military records burned, biometrics purged, and family ties severed.
   - He transforms into **Hamza Ali Mazari**, a ruthless Baloch gunrunner navigating the dark underbelly of Karachi.

3. **Chapter III: The Lyari Labyrinth (Rehman Dakait)**
   - Entering the blood-drenched gang wars of Chakiwara and Lyari.
   - Confronting crime lord **Rehman Dakait (Akshaye Khanna)** and earning his trust through high-stakes dock raids and gun battles at Kemari Harbor.

4. **Chapter IV: The Lawless Enforcer (S.P. Choudhary Aslam)**
   - Enter legendary encounter specialist **S.P. Choudhary Aslam (Sanjay Dutt)** with aviators, bullet smoke, and zero tolerance.
   - The volatile 3-way standoff between undercover spy, gangland kingpin, and rogue police juggernaut.

5. **Chapter V: The Shadow Master (Major Iqbal)**
   - De-encrypting the classified links between Lyari crime syndicate funding and **Major Iqbal (Arjun Rampal)** of the ISI external operations wing.
   - Uncovering the ticking blueprint for a coordinated multi-city strike on India.

6. **Chapter VI: Operation Dhurandhar & The Climax**
   - Sanyal gives the order: *"Unleash Dhurandhar"*.
   - Hamza breaks deep cover in an explosive harbor extraction firefight.
   - Setting the stage for Part 2: ***Dhurandhar: The Revenge*** (March 2026).

## ⚡ Interactive & Technical Highlights

- **Sticky Stage Scrollytelling:** As you scroll through the right-hand narrative cards, the pinned left-hand tactical monitor dynamically shifts modes, updating satellite pings, biometric scrubbers, urban gang territory maps, and power standoff diagrams.
- **Web Audio API Atmospheric Synthesizer:** Real-time synthesized 55Hz espionage drone, radio tape hiss, and sonar ping without needing external MP3 files. Includes an audio toggle with animated equalizer bars.
- **Live Canvas Tactical Radar:** 60fps radar sweep animation with dynamic range rings and wandering surveillance blips.
- **Interactive Classified Dossier Modal:** Detailed tactical profiles, combat stats, psychological assessments, and quotes for Ranveer Singh, Akshaye Khanna, Sanjay Dutt, R. Madhavan, Arjun Rampal, and Sara Arjun.
- **Clickable Redacted Documents:** Click blacked-out redactions (`TOP SECRET`) to unredact classified intel with authentic audio stamps.
- **Retro CRT Scanlines Overlay:** Toggle authentic vintage surveillance CRT monitor lines on or off.
- **Active RAW Command Terminal:** An interactive CLI prompt supporting commands like `status`, `cast`, `intel`, `director`, `part2`, `decrypt`, and `clear`.
